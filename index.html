01 / About

About

I'm an independent student developer and aspiring aerospace physicist, working where fundamental physics, engineering, technology and complex systems meet.

My work spans Unreal Engine 5 game development and world-building, systems architecture, robotics, and scientific data. I want to know how a system works at a fundamental level, connect ideas across disciplines, and turn them into structured projects I can check and improve.

Most recently I led a three-person team at the NASA Space Apps Challenge, where we built Flare AI, a tool that grades fire-safety evidence from NASA's microgravity combustion experiments.

02 / Projects / Flare AI

Flagship NASA Space Apps 2026 · Flame in Freefall

Flare AI

Evidence-graded fire safety · microgravity data

A research instrument for fire safety beyond Earth. Every combustion investigation in NASA's Physical Sciences Informatics archive, in one evidence base: each value traced to its source, every gap left visible. I led the team and directed the build end to end.

Role
Team lead · 628 Aerospace
Sources
BASS · BASS-II · FLEX · Saffire
Scenarios
Spacecraft · Lunar · Martian
Status
Deployed
Live site ↗ Source ↗ Demo ↗
  • 24Combustion investigations · NASA PSI archive
  • 537Test records · each verified by hand

System highlights

  1. 01

    Verified pipeline

    NASA test tables extracted in Python. Re-checked against the original reports on every build.

  2. 02

    Comparison engine

    Rankings and like-for-like comparisons that refuse mismatched conditions. A scenario matcher returns the nearest real tests.

  3. 03

    Grounded assistant

    Answers only from the record. Cites every claim. States when the data is silent.

  4. 04

    Hardened delivery

    Server-side keys, strict security policy, rate limits, automated browser tests.

Principle

No invented numbers. Every gap visible. Every claim cited.

Built through pull requests. AI coding tools directed; every output verified by hand.
